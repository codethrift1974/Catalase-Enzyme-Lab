AP Biology: Enzyme Activity (Potato Catalase Assay)

Floating Filter Paper Disc Bioassay • Reaction: 2H₂O₂ + Catalase → 2H₂O + O₂ + Catalase

Assay Configuration

  1. Keep potato catalase stock (100 units/mL) in an ice bath (4°C).
  2. Immerse 2.1 cm filter paper disc into enzyme solution for 5 seconds using forceps.
  3. Drain disc on paper towel for 10 seconds.
  4. Place disc at the bottom of the 40 mL H₂O₂ beaker and time until the disc floats flat on top of the solution.

Background & Assay Reference

In this investigation, you study the enzyme catalase, isolated from fresh potato tubers (50 g potato tissue homogenized in 50 mL cold distilled water + crushed ice, filtered through cheesecloth, brought to 100 mL total volume = 100 units/mL stock kept in an ice bath).

Catalase accelerates the breakdown of toxic hydrogen peroxide ($\text{H}_2\text{O}_2$) in peroxisomes according to the summary reaction:
$2\text{H}_2\text{O}_2 + \text{catalase} \rightarrow 2\text{H}_2\text{O} + \text{O}_2 + \text{catalase}$
